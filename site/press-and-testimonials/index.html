DEAR ARCHNA,

"By the way, I don't know if I have told you this… I tell all my friends and clients that you are the only Indian Restaurant in Atlanta worth going to. There are so many now, but none of them surpass your quality and style."

-Chitra

"Ram and I want to thank you on behalf of the entire wedding party and all the guests for dinner on June 23rd 2007. The menu selection was perfect. Thank you for helping us, your service was out of this world. You far exceeded our expectations. The compliments started prior to dinner when cocktails were served, we got so many inquiries on who did the catering for us. Compliments continued all through dinner. If I had to pick one favorite dish, I would be at a loss, every bite was mouth watering. From the kebabs and Chicken 65 for cocktails to the Tikka Masala during dinner… and oh the Rusmalai… divine. We are thankful to you and your staff for being so cooperative and on time, delivering in a professional manner and at the right temperature. It makes all the difference. Just so you know, we had guests from Germany, Japan, Spain, India, and New York, and they made comments such as "this is the best indian food we have ever had, it was so tender is just melted in
my mouth" and 'the best Rusmalai ever.'"

-Uma

"A big thank you for the wonderful arrangements for our event on May 22nd. I think everyone had a great time. The food of course was delicious, and up to everyone's expectation. As you well know, your cooking has developed a great name in the community, and I am quite certain that news about this Sat. will definitely add to your reputation. Poonam and Surendraji are wonderful, and it was a pleasure having them around as well. My husband was very impressed with the positive energy that you yourself had all evening."

-Shabnam

"I just wanted to let you know that your team did a fabulous job with the catering order. Eveyone had nothing but good things to say about the quality & quantity of the food that you supplied. -Dilip Jain

I will definitely recommend your restaurant to all my friends and relatives.

"I wanted to send an email of huge thanks to you and the staff at the Cooks Warehouse.  Our company party was talked about for days following this event.  The theme of an Iron Chef competition inspired my team to get competitive and do what they love most that is cooking. Who would have guessed that cooking was so popular but we could not have planned any better. All of my wannabee chef's got to show their stuff.

I think we had it all, those who played it safe and followed the recipe, those who were innovative and could care less about the recipe and of course those who set out to sabotage the other teams.  Between the drama, theatre and the wonderful food and wine it was clear that the entertainment value was outstanding and will not be forgotten.
