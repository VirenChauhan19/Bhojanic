The person behind the kitchen

It began with
her grandmother.

Before Bhojanic, there was a family kitchen in India. That’s where Archna learned to cook—and where her love of feeding people began.

More than 25 years in Atlanta’s food community later, those home-style traditions still shape the way we cook. Now they travel a little further: to weddings, workplaces, music cruises, and kitchens around the world.
